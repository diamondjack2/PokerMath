They're not pros, they have regular lifes and dont have or want to spend the time learning how to use complex solvers. They understand the game is mathematically and want to drill the maths directly.

A drill trainer that teaches you to estimate poker math live (while playing online or in person), and measures whether you're getting better at it. With explinations why.
